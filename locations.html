OVERVIEW

GOODLAND IN NEW YORK CITY

Goodland operates two indoor pickleball clubs in New York City.

Williamsburg sits on the Brooklyn waterfront with three courts, a café and bar, and a deal courts. Forest Hills sits on Queens Boulevard with seven courts for pickleball and badminton, a full bar and kitchen, and event space for up to 125 guests.

Both clubs are indoor and climate- controlled, use our propitiatory pro- grade cshion courts, and are open daily from 6am to midnight.

QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOU GO

How many Goodland locations are there in NYC?

Two: Williamsburg at Williamsburg Wharf in Brooklyn, and Forest Hills on Queens Boulevard in Queens. Both open in Late 2026.

Does one membership cover both clubs?

With a multi-access membership. A Goodland membership works at every NYC club.

Which club should I go to?

Williamsburg is in the thick of the action, great for regular, pros and corporate blocks. Forest Hills has six courts, badminton and a full bar and kitchen — best of beginners, intermediates and advanced players.
