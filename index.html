What Goodland Is

The Short Version

Goodland Pickleball is an indoor pickleball club operator in New York City.

We run two climate-controlled clubs — Williamsburg in Brooklyn and Forest Hills in Queens — with pro-grade acrylic courts, open play, ladder leagues, coaching, private court rentals, and private events with food and drinks.

Member First

Members and non-members can book a court online, walk in for open play, or take a buyout for a party of up to 125. We also design and build acrylic courts for other owners.
