What Goodland Is

The Short Version

Goodland Pickleball is an indoor pickleball club operator in New York City.

We run two climate-controlled clubs — Williamsburg in Brooklyn and Forest Hills in Queens — with pro-grade acrylic courts, open play, ladder leagues, coaching, private court rentals, and private events with food and drinks.

Member First

Members and non-members can book a court online, walk in for open play, or take a buyout for a party of up to 125. We also design and build acrylic courts for other owners.

QUESTIONS

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Where can I play indoor pickleball in NYC?

Goodland runs two indoor pickleball clubs in New York City: Williamsburg at Williamsburg Wharf in Brooklyn, and Forest Hills on Queens Boulevard in Queens. Both are indoor, climate-controlled and open year-round.

Do I need to be a member to play at Goodland?

No. Anyone can book a private court or drop into an open play session without a membership. Members pay one monthly price for unlimited open play, discounted leagues, a free clinic each month and 40% off private courts.

How much does it cost to play pickleball at Goodland?

Open play starts at $25 per person. Private courts start at $80 per hour and hold up to six players, with additional players at $25 per hour each. Memberships start at $149 per month and cover both clubs.

Can I book a court for a private event or corporate party?

Yes. Goodland hosts private events from 12 to 250 guests, including corporate offsites, brand activations, birthdays and team socials. Every buyout includes courts, paddles, a host who runs the games, and food and drink service.

Do you serve food and drinks?

Yes. Both clubs have a bar and kitchen on site, serving all day to players and guests. Private event packages include a catered menu and a drinks package.

Do you rent paddles?

Yes. Paddle rental is $5 per person and balls are complimentary at every Goodland location.

Is Goodland good for beginners?

Yes. Intro clinics run several times a week and need no experience or equipment. Open play sessions are sorted by skill level, so beginners play with beginners.

Do you build pickleball courts for other people?

Yes. Goodland Courts designs and builds 5-layer acrylic pickleball courts for developers, clubs, schools and private residences, indoors and outdoors.
